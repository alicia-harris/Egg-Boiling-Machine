# Automated Egg-Boiling Machine

## Overview

For EGR 101 at Duke University, my team designed and built a machine that
automated the egg-boiling process by rotating eggs between hot and cold water
every two minutes.

## My contributions

- Completed the system wiring
- Assisted with Arduino programming
- Designed the physical prototype
- Helped troubleshoot electrical and mechanical interactions
- Participated in testing and iteration

## Technologies

- Arduino
- C/C++
- Electrical wiring
- Prototyping
- Mechanical design
- Sensors and timing control

## How it works

1. The system places the egg in hot water.
2. After two minutes, the mechanism moves or rotates the egg.
3. The egg is transferred between hot and cold water according to the programmed cycle.
4. The process repeats to produce a consistent result.
